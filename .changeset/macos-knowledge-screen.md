**Knowledge is in the Mac app.** The Knowledge row opens screen 10: the newest fold in the assistant's own words, with its page names as links; Needs your eye — drafts, conflicts and suggestions — whose drafts and suggestions open the Needs You card itself, so answering one here answers it there; a conflict's diff with Keep Mine and Take the Other held ten seconds under Undo before anything is sent; Areas with their written line and why each is in front of you; one sources line that says *freshness unknown* rather than infer *current*; and, behind them, an area's pages, search (⌘F) and a page with its outgoing and incoming links.

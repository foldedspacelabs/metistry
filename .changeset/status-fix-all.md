The Status window gains **Fix All** (X-125) — the Mac half of `metistry doctor`'s repair contract (X-123), and a closed-set action (invariant 10): the CLI door `doctor --fix --yes --json`, nothing else. It is offered only while doctor itself has classified a row `fixable` and named its repair; a your-hand row shows what to do and has no button, and a CLI that does not classify rows gets no Fix All. It re-reads first, then runs at once if nothing in the plan moves files or restarts a running service, or otherwise puts a confirm on screen naming every fix (the ones that ask marked) — `--yes` leaves the app only from that sheet's Fix All, and Cancel, Esc and dismissing it send nothing. M6 now admits exactly two argvs — `doctor --check --json` (which every read of doctor now runs, read-only whatever the terminal) and `doctor --fix --yes --json` — instead of any argv beginning `doctor`. The answer is drawn as one line, each fix with the statuses either side of its re-check and the CLI's own ledger, and each row says *fixed* or *still failing*; a fix that asked without being in the confirmed preview is said afterwards, and a CLI that ignored `--fix` is reported as that, not as "nothing needed fixing". Tests: Fix All runs the fixable rows only; a destructive fix asks first and Cancel sends nothing; every other doctor argv is refused; §2.18 for the panel and the sheet.

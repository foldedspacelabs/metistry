**Today's top on the Mac (T6-1b).** The Today row now opens the day. The Morning Brief is its first state — one serif paragraph with the configured name and *written, not retrieved*, the Standup collapsed with Copy Standup — and folds to its first sentence on the next open. From thirty minutes before a meeting, Next Up shows who is in it, what you owe them (tickable), the brief's line for it, Open Notes, Record (dimmed until the capture bar) and Draft the Agenda. When no focus block is left, calendar help offers one move, and a meeting with other people in it warns first, naming who the calendar will tell and the new time. From thirty minutes before the working day ends, Close the Day: done, still open and owed lines each with Tomorrow · This Week · Someday, tomorrow's shape, a line for tomorrow, and a folded *Day closed at…* line — or, when the daily note's markers are broken, the request, never a success.

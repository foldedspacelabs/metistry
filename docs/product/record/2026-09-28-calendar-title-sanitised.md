- 2026-09-28 — **An invite's title cannot write into the owner's Journal.** `{{ calendar }}` renders one line per event, and until now that line trusted whoever sent the invite: a title carrying a newline could open a markdown heading, a frontmatter delimiter or a task checkbox on its own line, and one carrying `[[…]]` or `<…>` could complete a wikilink or slip in raw HTML. The engine now collapses every line break before it renders and breaks the two token forms that don't need one, so the whole event still fits on its one line and the worst a hostile invite can do is sit there as inert text (Ruling 16).

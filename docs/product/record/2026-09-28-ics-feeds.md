- 2026-09-28 — **Any published calendar joins Today, without a second calendar table.** An ICS feed is a connection (`metistry connections add … --provider ics --url …`), and its sync expands recurrences and time zones — VTIMEZONE rules for Outlook's zone names, DST kept on the wall clock, all-day dates on the owner's own day — into the same `calendar_events` the Mac's calendar fills, keyed the way EventKit keys an occurrence so a moved meeting keeps its note. It reaches only the feed's own host through the egress door, follows no redirect, and never reads an invite body. A feed address that carries a token (Google's secret address, a published iCloud calendar) is refused as a key before it is written anywhere; how a secret that *is* a URL may be sent is left to the owner (T4-12).

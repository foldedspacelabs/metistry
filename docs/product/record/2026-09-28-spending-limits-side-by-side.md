- 2026-09-28 — **Every spending limit is one read, and a subscription is limited by its plan, not by dollars.** The owner's limits used to live in two places that never met: the instance's and each provider's in `compute.yaml`, each project's daily budget in Postgres behind the Projects pane. `GET /api/compute` now answers with all of them side by side, each beside what has been spent against it, so Settings › Compute › Spending limits can show the whole picture in one list. A provider billed by subscription costs nothing per call beyond its plan, so a dollar limit on it could never fire and would only look like a control; the schema now refuses one on every door, and the provider's line shows the calls made in each window instead — its plan's window is its limit (C133).

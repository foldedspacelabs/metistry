- 2026-09-30 — **Needs You now holds the invitations and the mail that wait on the owner — and answering them sends nothing the owner did not see first.** A meeting the owner has not answered, organised by someone else, is one card per meeting whichever calendars hold it, and it leaves by itself when the owner answers anywhere, the meeting is cancelled or it passes; Accept, Maybe or Decline previews who the reply goes to, then answers through the calendar that can — and where none can (the Mac's own calendar is read-only), Metistry says so and offers Open in Calendar instead of pretending. A message becomes a card only when its headers say it asks something of the owner — written to them, by a person, unanswered, a reply or to them alone — and the card says it was inferred from the headers, because no message body is ever fetched: nothing of what the mail says reaches the assistant or an agent. Draft Reply writes the reply into the owner's Drafts, addressed from the message itself, never from the caller; the owner sends it. Proven against local CalDAV and IMAP servers that record every request (T4-17).

- 2026-09-30 — **Purge Now names its cost before it deletes anything, on both
  panes.** Settings ▸ Sessions reads what a purge would cost — every archived
  session, and the ones the assistant has not learned from yet — and pressing
  Purge Now opens a confirm that lists those unfolded sessions and offers *Fold
  First* instead; only the confirmed call deletes, and it carries the preview's
  own timestamp so a turn archived while the dialog was open is kept. Settings
  ▸ Live Capture does the same for recordings: the amount of audio still kept
  sits beside the button, and the confirm names each recording that goes while
  its transcript stays. Both are enforced where they run — a confirm that
  names no cost cannot be built, and tests hold each button to the wire: Purge
  Now pressed sends nothing, Cancel sends nothing, Fold First runs the fold and
  never the purge. The pane also makes the recorder's permissions a read-through
  (*Approved* or *Not yet asked*, from the recorder's own report) rather than a
  control that pretends to grant them, and turns the bar's switch and placement
  into the one device-local setting the app stores.

- 2026-09-30 — **A recording's audio is gone within 30 days, and "check the audio again" answers in words.** The Mac that recorded a meeting keeps its audio until the transcript has been dealt with — the meeting's proposals decided, or the fold has read it — plus 7 days, and never more than 30 days after the recording ended; it enforces that on its own hourly clock, so a console that is down or rebuilt cannot keep audio longer, and the one thing the console may report (when the transcript was ingested) can only bring a deletion forward. Transcripts are filed under `Journal/Transcripts/` in the owner's name, outside every default grant, and deleted as a commit on their 30th day with their words cleared from the database too. The assistant can ask to re-hear a span — `recording_review` re-transcribes up to 15 minutes on the Mac and returns text with timestamps, never audio; no crew can hold it; after deletion it says when the audio went and that the transcript remains. Settings reads it all from `GET /api/recordings/:id`.

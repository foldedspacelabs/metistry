- 2026-10-18 — **Every collector now answers for itself in `metistry doctor`, and a broken one costs only its own row.** The convention that each collector exports `check()` was written down and half-built: the Devin collectors had one and nothing asked it, and ten others had none. Now all twelve report whether the next scheduled run could reach what it reads — configuration, credential and connection state, asked cheaply and locally, never a full sync and never a billed call (Cost Explorer is not touched) — and doctor lays the answer over the collector's single row. A module that will not load, a check that throws, or one that hangs is a red row with the reason while the rest of the report prints; a collector with a model tier pointed at an off-machine provider is found before the unattended job spends. One CI test fails any collector added without a check, with no list to add it to (X-87).

- 2026-10-18 — **Removing a device now removes it: its passkey is revoked, not just a session, so a lost phone cannot sign back in.** The website's "you can remove it from Devices anytime" is true as of this change (design-build-plan §2.22, X-76). A device is a passkey with its sessions under it, and Remove — `POST /api/devices/:id/revoke` — marks the passkey revoked (`passkeys.revoked_at`, migration 0038, durable like a session's), ends every session under it and drops their push subscriptions in the same act, so its notifications stop too. A revoked passkey answers a login exactly as an unknown credential does — the same uniform 401, no cookie — so nothing in the refusal says whether the device ever existed; and a session is checked against its passkey on every request, so one issued in the instant between a login's lookup and the Remove dies with the device. Sign Out Everywhere keeps its meaning (sessions only, every passkey kept) as the same door with `scope: "sessions"`. Every Remove and sign-out lands in `runs` with the scope, the passkey and the number of sessions ended, and the misuse tests hold the two doors to the owner's credentials alone.

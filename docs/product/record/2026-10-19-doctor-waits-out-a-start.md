- 2026-10-19 — **Doctor stops crying wolf at the moment you update.** Twice on 2026-10-04 the one command that exists to say whether an install is healthy said it was not, when it was: right after an update it reported sixteen scheduled jobs as not being run, because the console had been restarted seconds earlier and its scheduler had not had its first minute yet; and right after `up` it reported the recorder down, because the helper behind it had been started a second before. Doctor now knows what it has just started. A bridge or the console that is still coming up is asked again, every second, for a bounded grace before it is called failed; a scheduled job that is late only because its scheduler has not ticked yet reads as *pending*, with why. The line is held both ways in tests: a job that was not just started, or whose age cannot be told, is still reported failed on the first answer, with no benefit of the doubt. The grace is a setting, not a guess, and `up` and `update` hand doctor the exact moment of each restart. It is the first half of "run `metistry doctor` and it fixes it": a repair is only safe once the thing it repairs is not a false reading.

- 2026-10-19 — **The Mac can now tell the owner that the phone reached it — from outside — before anything has been added, so "it doesn't work" stops being a silent wait.** Setting up remote access ends in a QR the owner scans, and until now a phone that could not reach the Mac and a phone that had not been picked up looked the same: a code counting down to nothing. The console now notes, on the one-time code itself, the moment the phone's first request for it arrives and which door it came through — the one a provider forwards to, or the Mac's own — and the *Add a Phone* sheet shows *Opened on your phone* only for the first: proof from the outside that this address works, which the Mac cannot prove about itself. The owner opening the link on the Mac cannot fake that proof and cannot hide the real one, noting it never uses the code up, and a guessed, spent, expired or cancelled code leaves no note. If the ten minutes pass without it, the sheet says the phone never reached the address and what to try (Safari, Wi-Fi off), instead of leaving the owner to guess which of the provider, the name or the phone failed.

# Working style

> Metistry discovers how you want to work from what you write here — it
> never assumes a prioritisation rule or a standup shape for you. The two
> sections below are included, word for word, into the plan and the
> standup draft (`{{ include }}`, `docs/product/daily-flow-spec.md` §6.6);
> write them in your own words whenever you're ready. Until you do, what
> is below is only an example, not a rule Metistry is using.

## Prioritisation

(For example: prioritise anything due tomorrow, then everything P1, then
as many small tasks as fit the day. Deep work before the first meeting.
Never more than one large task on a day with two or more meetings.)

## Standup

(For example: yesterday / today / blockers. Skip anything that is not in
a shared repo.)
